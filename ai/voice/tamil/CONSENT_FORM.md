# Voice Recording Consent Form — MindAura Tamil Emotional Speech Corpus

**Project:** MindAura — an AI tool that helps psychologists monitor patients'
emotional wellbeing. This recording will be used to train the part of the system
that recognises emotion from tone of voice.

**What you will do:** Read ~20 short, ordinary Tamil sentences aloud, each in a
few different emotional tones (angry, happy, sad, afraid, neutral). Takes about
20 minutes. Only your **voice** is recorded — no video, no photo.

**What is stored:** The audio clips, plus your gender, age range, and native
region. **Your name is not stored** — you are identified only by a code (e.g.
"S03").

  models**, including in a **commercial product** (MindAura).
- ___ I agree the recordings and the basic metadata above may be **shared or
  published as a research/development dataset**.
- ___ I understand my name is not stored and the recordings cannot easily be
  linked back to me.
- ___ I understand I can ask for my recordings to be deleted at any time before
  the dataset is published, by contacting the project team.
